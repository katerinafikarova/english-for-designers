# Letterform: Uppercase K - Process

Honey gingerbread cookie, carved by hand, baked and glazed.

Kateřina Fikarová, 20 October 2026

![The final uppercase K: glazed gingerbread on a white plate](00-final-k-plate.jpg)

## Why K?

Only straight lines, triangles that pull out easily, and the first letter of my name.

Rounded letters fall apart when you carve them from dough. I know this from earlier experience.

## 01 Recipe and ingredients

I found the recipe and prepared the ingredients.

Then I melted the butter with the sugar and honey.

> I got afraid that I was going to burn it.

![Ingredients laid out next to a laptop with the recipe](01a-recipe-ingredients.jpg)

![Butter and sugar melting in a small saucepan](01b-butter-sugar-melting.jpg)

## 02 Mixing the dough

I put flour, eggs and spices into a bowl and mixed them with the honey mixture.

I mixed it by hand in gloves. I have baked a lot, and this was not how a good dough feels.

> It felt like kinetic sand.

![Flour, spices and an egg in a bowl on a scale](02a-flour-spices-egg.jpg)

![Gloved hand mixing crumbly dough](02b-mixing-dough.jpg)

## 03 Freezer and cleaning

The dough had to go into the freezer for a while.

Meanwhile I cleaned the kitchen, so I could move on to the next step.

![Wrapped ball of dough in the freezer](03a-dough-in-freezer.jpg)

![Messy kitchen counter after mixing](03b-messy-kitchen.jpg)

![Clean kitchen with pastry mat and rolling pin](03c-clean-kitchen.jpg)

## 04 Rolling out

I rolled out the dough.

For carving, I took special tools for cake making and decorating.

![Rolled-out dough on a pastry mat](04a-rolled-out-dough.jpg)

![Hand holding a cake decorating tool above the dough](04b-decorating-tool.jpg)

## 05 Carving the letters

I was really scared of this step. Letters fall apart, and rounded ones are hard to cut.

The K worked: straight cuts, and the triangles came out easily. The first two went onto the baking sheet.

![Two K outlines carved into the dough](05a-two-k-outlines.jpg)

![Two cut-out Ks with scraps pushed aside](05b-cut-out-ks.jpg)

![Two raw Ks on baking paper](05c-raw-ks-on-paper.jpg)

## 06 Filling the sheet

I carved Ks until I ran out of dough or space on the baking sheet.

With the rest of the dough, I cut little circles and filled the empty places.

![Several Ks carved close together](06a-ks-close-together.jpg)

![Pastry mat with a small round cutter](06b-round-cutter.jpg)

![Full baking sheet of raw Ks and circles](06c-full-baking-sheet.jpg)

## 07 Baking

**180 °C, 10 minutes**

While it baked, I cleaned the whole kitchen again. I like to clean while something is in the oven.

![Baking sheet of Ks and circles in the oven](07-baking-in-oven.jpg)

## 08 Glaze and decoration

No powdered sugar left, and too much egg white and lemon juice: the glaze became slippery and liquid.

I had little time, so I drew it across the cookies with a spoon, back and forth.

> Splotchy, splashy shapes.

![Thin white glaze in a glass with a spoon](08a-thin-glaze.jpg)

![Hand drizzling glaze over the baked Ks](08b-drizzling-glaze.jpg)

## 09 Tasting, cooling, choosing

Before decorating, I tasted them. Very delicious.

When the sheet was done, it went into the fridge to cool.

Then I chose the best-looking K.

![Glazed Ks on baking paper in the fridge](09-glazed-ks-fridge.jpg)

## The final letterform

![The final uppercase K: glazed gingerbread on a white plate](00-final-k-plate.jpg)

- **Made by:** Kateřina Fikarová
- **Material:** Honey gingerbread, egg-white and lemon glaze
- **Technique:** Carved by hand, baked at 180 °C, glazed with a spoon

## What I learned

### Why this K

I chose this K because it was the most symmetrical and the best baked of them all.

### A limit that gave me room to play

Time pressure. I did not have much time, so I had to be as efficient as possible. It was still fun, because I love baking.
